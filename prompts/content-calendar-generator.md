# 📅 Content Calendar Generator

A **Generative AI powered module** that automatically generates a **structured weekly content calendar** based on a selected **topic, audience, and platform**.

This tool helps creators, marketers, and businesses maintain **consistent posting, audience engagement, and strategic content planning**.

---

# 🎯 System Purpose

The **Content Calendar Generator** helps content creators organize their weekly posting strategy by automatically generating a **balanced mix of educational, engagement, insight, and value-driven content**.

This ensures:

✔ Consistent content publishing  
✔ Stronger audience engagement  
✔ Strategic content planning  
✔ Faster workflow for creators

# ⚙️ Prompt Design

## ROLE

You are a **professional content strategist and digital marketing expert** specializing in **content growth and audience engagement**.

---

## OBJECTIVE

Generate a **structured weekly content calendar** designed to **attract, educate, and engage the target audience** on the selected platform.

---

## INPUT VARIABLES

Topic: `[Business or Topic]`  
Audience: `[Target Audience]`  
Platform: `[LinkedIn / Instagram / Twitter / Blog]`

---

## INSTRUCTIONS

1. Generate a **weekly content calendar**.
2. Include **5–7 posts** for the week.
3. Use a **balanced mix of content types**:

- Educational
- Engagement
- Insight
- Value

4. Ensure ideas are **platform relevant**.
5. Each content idea must include:

- Content Title  
- Content Type  
- Short Description
