🚀 Content Idea Generator
Overview

The Content Idea Generator helps creators, marketers, and businesses generate high-quality and engaging content ideas tailored to their audience and platform.
It uses structured prompt engineering techniques to produce ideas that are relevant, practical, and optimized for audience engagement.

🎯 Objective

Generate creative, relevant, and engaging content ideas based on a specific topic, target audience, and platform.

The goal is to help users quickly discover valuable content topics that can drive engagement, visibility, and audience growth.

📥 Input Variables

Business / Topic	The main topic, product, or industry
Target Audience	The people the content is meant for
Platform	Where the content will be published (LinkedIn, Instagram, Blog, etc.)
